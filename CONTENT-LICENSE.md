## The purpose of splitting the license

The application code is offered under permissive MIT terms because there's
little downside to broad, unrestricted reuse of the codebase itself. The
guide content on the other hand represents countless hours of research, education,
experience, and technical expertise. This structure allows for content to
be shared freely and not gatekept.
